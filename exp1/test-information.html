Diagnostic Process & Test Parameters

The Urine Test Strip Reader automates the optical evaluation of diagnostic reagents. This system assists medical staff in detecting metabolic abnormalities, kidney conditions, and suspected UTI cases.

Key Test Parameters

The digital reader analyzes several key indicators on each reagent pad:

  • Glucose: Measured in units of mg/dL (expressed as mg·dL-1 or mg/dL). High levels may indicate hyperglycemia.
  • Leukocytes (WBC): Evaluated to identify inflammation or active bacterial infection.
  • Erythrocytes (RBC): Scanned for intact blood cells or free hemoglobin.
  • Acidity (pH): Evaluates urinary balance, with normal ranges typically varying between 4.5 and 8.0. Hydronium ion activity depends on H3O+ concentrations.
  • Specific Gravity (SG): Measures fluid concentration and kidney hydration efficiency relative to pure H2O.

Healthcare Instructions

Always ensure the urine sample is fresh and analyzed within one hour of collection. Do not touch the reagent pads directly, and store test strip containers tightly sealed at room temperature.

Test Information & Diagnostic Process

The Urine Test Strip Reader automates the optical evaluation of multi-parameter chemical strips. The diagnostic process aids in detecting metabolic conditions, renal diseases, and UTI quickly.

Important Test Parameters

The optical reader measures color changes for critical biochemical analytes:

  • Glucose: Measured in units of mg/dL, often represented scientifically as mg·dL-1. Elevated glucose levels indicate possible diabetes mellitus.
  • Leukocytes (WBC): Indicates the presence of white blood cells in urine, a primary sign of bacterial infection.
  • Erythrocytes / Blood (RBC): Detects intact red blood cells or free hemoglobin released due to glomerular damage or calculi.
  • Acidity (pH): Measures urinary hydrogen ion concentration, reflecting the activity of H3O+ ions. Normal values range from 4.5 to 8.0.
  • Specific Gravity (SG): Measures urine ionic concentration relative to pure distilled H2O. Typical normal range is 1.005 to 1.030.
  • Protein: Assesses albumin concentration in mg·dL-1. Detects early signs of chronic kidney disease.

Healthcare Instructions

Always use a freshly collected midstream urine specimen tested within one hour of collection.
Ensure the reagent strip is dipped for exactly one second and excess urine is blotted on an absorbent paper without touching the test pads.
Store reagent strip containers tightly capped at temperatures between 15°C and 30°C, away from direct sunlight and moisture.

Medical Disclaimer

Medical Disclaimer: Automated urine test strip reader outputs are intended for preliminary screening and diagnostic assistance only. Test results should be interpreted by a licensed physician in conjunction with complete clinical findings, medical history, and confirmatory laboratory investigations.

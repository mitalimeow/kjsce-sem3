Objectives

The primary objective of this reader is to provide standardized diagnostic screening across all clinical departments. By replacing manual color comparison charts, the system minimizes inter-observer variability, ensures high diagnostic accuracy, and logs patient records directly into the electronic database using HL7 and FHIR protocols.

It supports point-of-care rapid testing to help clinicians identify kidney dysfunction, diabetes, and systemic urinary tract infections within 60 seconds.

Working Principle

The device works on the principle of reflectance photometry. A calibrated light-emitting diode illuminates each chemical reagent pad on the urine strip. The reflected light intensity is detected by an optical sensor and converted into digital values.

The onboard microprocessor compares measured reflectance values against standard calibration curves to compute analyte concentrations accurately. The process follows the standard equation Reflectance (R) = (Sample Signal / Reference Signal) × 100%.

Benefits

Healthcare providers gain multiple clinical advantages:

1. Rapid Turnaround: Results are calculated in less than one minute.
2. Error Reduction: Eliminates subjective color perception by different human operators.
3. Data Traceability: Every patient record is timestamped with operator ID and strip lot number.
4. Portability: Lightweight design allows convenient bedside and outpatient testing.

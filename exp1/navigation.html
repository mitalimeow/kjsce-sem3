Internal Portal Pages

Access all modules and functional interfaces of the Urine Test Strip Reader Portal:

  • Home Page (Task 1) — Document structure, global attributes, and system overview.
  • Test Information Page (Task 2) — Diagnostic formatting, chemical formulas, and medical abbreviations.
  • About System Page (Task 3) — Objectives, working principle, benefits, and doctor endorsement.
  • Test Parameters Page (Task 4) — Testing procedures, 10 analytes, stakeholder roles, and abbreviation dictionary.
  • Navigation Page (Task 5) — Site directory, external links, email, and emergency telephone links.

  • Media & Hospital Information Page (Task 9) — Video guidance, audio advisory, map iframe, and noscript alert.
